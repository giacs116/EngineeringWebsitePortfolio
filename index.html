About Me

A portfolio centered on engineering clarity.

My name is Alexander Giacomodonato and I am a 19 year-old Mechatronics Engineering student in my first year at the University of Waterloo with a strong interest in both software programming and mechanical design. I enjoy building practical, well-designed systems that combine thoughtful engineering with clean user experiences.

My programming strengths include problem solving, UI/UX design, debugging, and code optimization. I am motivated to continue developing technically rigorous and impactful solutions at the intersection of software and hardware.

Outside of school, I am a driven and active individual who enjoys staying physically active through sports and movement, including squash, rock climbing, badminton, and pickleball.
